# Core places

From the per-category research in `places/research/`. ★ = already in the game. Confidence medium is marked (m).

## Torg (13)

- ★ **Stortorget**: Malmö's 1530s main square with the town hall and Karl X Gustav statue, the city's reference point.
- ★ **Lilla Torg**: The half-timbered square of outdoor dining that every Malmö resident knows and meets at.
- ★ **Gustav Adolfs torg**: Central square on the main shopping axis and a major bus stop; 'vid Gustav Adolfs torg' is everyday direction-giving.
- ★ **Möllevångstorget**: Möllan: daily market, Arbetets ära monument, the heart of Möllevången nightlife.
- ★ **Drottningtorget**: Large square on the northeast edge of the old town by Caroli, a well-known landmark and bus stop.
- ★ **Värnhemstorget**: Major bus interchange and market square east of the centre.
- ★ **Davidshallstorg**: Square at the heart of the Davidshall quarter, a common meeting point with cafés and restaurants.
- **Nobeltorget**: Busy junction square between Möllevången, Sofielund and Rosengård with a well-known bus stop.
- **Dalaplan**: Large traffic square and bus hub on the south side of the inner city that everyone uses as a landmark; currently being rebuilt as a proper square.
- **Fridhemstorget** (m): Market square and bus stop on the west side, the everyday meeting point for Fridhem/Västra Innerstaden.
- **Sankt Knuts torg** (m): Large square between Möllevången and Sofielund with a well-known bus stop, commonly used for directions.
- **Triangeln**: The square at Triangeln (Södra Förstadsgatan, by S:t Johannes kyrka and the station/mall) is listed by malmo.se among Malmö's squares ('Triangeltorget'); tagged highway=pedestrian so it fell out of the list.
- **Södervärn**: Bus-hub square south of the centre, already in the game as a square (landmarks.json); only the bus station is mapped, no square polygon.

> Core = 11 from the list plus Triangeln and Södervärn from missing (13 total). Fewer than 15 because beyond these the squares are neighbourhood-level. Borderline core calls for the owner: Limhamns torg (centre of Limhamn, well known locally but less city-wide), Kirsebergs torg (malmo.se sommartorg 2026), Anna Lindhs plats (by Malmö C, but people say 'Centralen'). Fridhemstorget and Sankt Knuts torg are medium: well-known bus stops/market squares, weaker guide signals. Stortorget's Wikidata item (Q8711309, 8 sitelinks) sits in _unmatched wikidataOnly; it should be linked to the OSM Stortorget. Triangeln is also a station (Triangelns station) and mall; if the game has it as a station, the square question may be redundant. Södervärn is already in landmarks.json as a square but has no square geometry in OSM, only the bus station node. Dalaplan is under reconstruction into a real square; it stays a landmark either way. Västra Hamnen plazas (Dockplatsen, Masttorget, Kockums torg, plus Scaniaplatsen in missing and Stapelplatsen relation/4663162 not added) have low recognition.

## Parker och natur (19)

- ★ **Kungsparken**: Malmö's oldest park (1872), central, next to the castle and Malmö Live.
- ★ **Pildammsparken**: Malmö's largest and best-known park around the Pildammarna ponds, a default meeting place.
- ★ **Folkets park**: Sweden's oldest folkets park (1891) at Möllevången, a fixed point for everyone in the city.
- ★ **Slottsparken**: Large central park by Malmöhus and the city library, one of the most used walks.
- ★ **Scaniaparken**: Waterfront park in Västra hamnen by the Scaniabadet jetties.
- ★ **Beijers park**: 12-hectare landscape park in Kirseberg, known from SVT's Sommarlov.
- **Slottsträdgården**: The garden next to Slottsparken and the windmill, with its café, widely known and visited.
- ★ **Bulltofta rekreationsområde**: Malmö's largest recreation area, on the old Bulltofta airport.
- ★ **Rörsjöparken** (m): 19th-century park in Rörsjöstaden just east of the centre, a common landmark.
- **Kroksbäcksparken** (m): Large 1970s park between Kroksbäck and Holma, a district name in its own right.
- **Jesusparken** (m): Falsterboplan, universally called Jesusparken, at Möllevången; 1.2k yearly sv views.
- **Rönneholmsparken** (m): 'Rönnan', the park around Rönneholmsgården, known across western inner city.
- **Klagshamnsuddens naturreservat** (m): Klagshamns udde, the nature area and bird spot on the old lime works fill; already in the game.
- ★ **Daniaparken**: Sea park in Västra hamnen with the most used diving jetties in the city.
- **Hyllie vattenpark** (m): Water play park in Hyllie, a summer destination for families from all of Malmö.
- ★ **Limhamns kalkbrott**: Huge former limestone quarry, now a nature reserve (protected_area, not leisure=park, so missed); in the game already, 5 Wikidata sitelinks.
- ★ **Öresundsparken**: The long shore park behind Ribersborgsstranden; on malmo.se history list and in the game. Only the western part is in the long list (Öresundsparken, västra, way/530533698).
- ★ **Sibbarp (Sibbarps badpark)**: Park and beach area at the bridge with kallbadhus, skatepark and camping; on malmo.se history list; Wikidata types it as park. OSM only has the admin area.
- ★ **Katrinetorp**: Empire-style landeri with historic gardens and English landscape park, Wikivoyage see; known for events and Christmas market.

Limhamns kalkbrott and Öresundsparken are in the game already but absent from the park long list; Lindängelund is the city's biggest park project but has no OSM polygon, so it would need an outline built from Millennieskogen (way/55801342) + Engelska parken (way/30037703) or left out. Jesusparken = Falsterboplan; I kept the northern half under the popular name and dropped the southern half. Alnarpsparken (Lomma) is well visited by Malmö people but kept in everything, outsideMalmo. Saltholm (Danish island) is in everything only as a visible landmark; drop it if Denmark should stay out. Borderline core candidates left in everything: Garnisonsplanteringen ('Tjyvaparken'), Gyllins trädgård, Käglinge naturområde, Ögårdsparken, Magistratsparken, Raoul Wallenbergs park, Bunkeflo strandängar.

## Bad och strand (8)

- **Ribersborgsstranden**: Ribban is Malmö's most visited beach, the city's default summer spot and a universal reference point.
- ★ **Ribersborgs Kallbadhus**: The 1898 cold-bath house on its pier at Ribban, 'Kallis', is one of Malmö's best known buildings and open all year.
- **Hylliebadet**: Malmö's main public indoor bath since 2015, next to Kroksbäcksparken by Hyllie.
- **Simhallsbadet** (m): The 1956 swimming hall at Regementsgatan, Aq-va-kul 1988-2015, known to generations of Malmö residents.
- **Scaniabadet**: Västra hamnen's popular bathing deck with piers and diving off Scaniaparken, one of the city's five official bathing places.
- **Klagshamnstranden** (m): The natural beach out on Klagshamnsudden, one of Malmö's five official bathing places.
- **Sibbarpsstranden**: Official bathing place at Sibbarp with piers, Barnviken and camping; in the game as 'Sibbarps badplats'. No beach polygon in OSM, so the osm id is the Sibbarp place node the game already uses.
- **Sibbarps kallbadhus (Sibbarps saltsjöbad)**: Malmö's second cold-bath house, open all year, on the pier at Sibbarp; listed in wikivoyage as Sibbarps Saltsjöbad and has its own sv Wikipedia article.

> Malmö lists exactly five official bathing places: Ribersborg, Sibbarp, Klagshamn, Scaniabadet, Sundspromenaden. It lists indoor baths Hylliebadet, Simhallsbadet and Oxievångsbadet, and outdoor pools Rosengårdsbadet, Lindängsbadet and Segevångsbadet. Simhallsbadet is core with medium confidence: it is the best known bath name in the city (ex-Aq-va-kul), but it has served only clubs and lap swimmers since 2015, the diving pool has been closed since March 2026 pending review, and Heleneholmsbadet (due autumn 2028) may take over its role. Demote it if the 'still there' test matters most. Sibbarp: Sibbarpsstranden and Sibbarps kallbadhus are listed separately, but the game may want one 'Sibbarp' item since they sit 300 m apart. Sundspromenaden is a street, and it may clash with the streets quiz. Left out: Aq-va-kul (old name of Simhallsbadet), Kockum Fritid (private gym with a pool, node/8602130734), Lagunen (way/450127262, a marina and area beside the nudist bathing spot, not a bathing place), Gamla Burlövs badet (way/356623631, the old building beside Burlövsbadet), Ribban's numbered piers (Brygga 1-10). There is no Limhamns kallbadhus and no 'Daniabadet' in OSM or on malmo.se. Lomma 

## Scener och nöje (19)

- ★ **Malmö Opera**: One of the Nordic region's largest opera houses, on Östra Rönneholmsvägen since 1944; the city's flagship stage.
- ★ **Malmö Live Konserthus**: The city's concert hall in the Malmö Live complex by the Central Station, home of Malmö SymfoniOrkester.
- ★ **Malmö Stadsteater (Hipp)**: The city theatre's main house, the Hippodromen circus building on Kalendegatan, known to everyone as Hipp.
- **Moriska Paviljongen**: The seven-towered 1902 pavilion in Folkets park, a concert and club venue known as Moriskan.
- **Spegeln**: Art-house cinema on Stortorget since 1934 (originally Alcazar), still running.
- **Royal**: Filmstaden's big 1961 cinema at Södra Tullgatan/Triangeln, Malmö's largest screen.
- **Filmstaden Storgatan** (m): The central multiplex on Storgatan since 1998, a common meeting point near Gustav Adolfs torg.
- **Victoriateatern** (m): 1912 cinema turned theatre and music stage on Södra Förstadsgatan, still active.
- **Palladium** (m): 1920 culture house on Södergatan between Stortorget and Gustav Adolfs torg, run as a concert venue by Musik i Syd.
- **Nöjesteatern**: Revue and musical stage at Amiralsgatan by Folkets park, a 1940 cinema building.
- **Babel** (m): Club and concert venue in a former church on Spångatan at Möllan.
- **Inkonst** (m): Long-running club and performance venue on Bergsgatan, home of Intonal festival.
- **Pildammsteatern**: The open-air stage in Pildammsparken used every summer since 1952.
- ★ **Malmö konsthall**: Major public art hall on S:t Johannesgatan since 1975, about 200,000 visitors a year.
- **Panora** (m): Folkets Bio's art-house cinema at Möllan, active in 2026.
- **Etage** (m): The multi-floor nightclub on Stortorget, running for about four decades.
- **Skånes Dansteater** (m): The regional dance company's house at Dockan in Västra hamnen.
- **Slagthuset**: Former slaughterhouse behind Centralen, a big concert, theatre and event venue (Slagthuset Teatern/Saluhallen); took over KB's bookings.
- **Amiralen**: 1939 dance palace in Folkets park, now shows, concerts and stand-up run with Nöjesteatern; no named OSM object, shares Amiralsgatan 35 with Nöjesteatern.

> Closed, so not added: Kulturbolaget/KB (Bergsgatan, closed Sept 2023; the stale OSM node/445847413 is still tagged nightclub and should not be used), Casino Cosmopol (Kungsparken, closed 24 Feb 2024), Debaser Malmö (left Folkets park 2013), old Malmö Konserthus (way/289662618, replaced by Malmö Live). Palladium had a lease dispute but still books concerts into 2026-27; recheck before shipping. Malmö konsthall is a gallery and should probably move to museums. Malmö Stadsteater's point is the Hipp building; the Wikidata item is in _unmatched (Q18244214), and way/417455554 Hippodromen gives the building outline. Malmö Arena (concerts, in game) and Folkets park (in game) are left to other categories. Mejeriet (Lund) lies outside the area. With so few long-list items, core is 17 + 2 missing = 19; Etage, Skånes Dansteater, Panora and Filmstaden Storgatan are the weakest core picks.

## Museer och bibliotek (8)

- ★ **Malmö Stadsbibliotek**: The main city library: Slottet plus Henning Larsen's Ljusets kalender, by Kungsparken. Everyone knows it and uses it as a landmark.
- ★ **Malmö museum (Slottsholmen / Malmöhus)**: The city museum in Malmöhus castle, with the aquarium, natural history and city history. It is the museum most Malmö residents have been to.
- ★ **Teknikens och sjöfartens hus**: The technology and maritime museum opposite Malmöhus, the classic school-trip museum. The tram and bus stop is still called Tekniska museet.
- ★ **Moderna Museet Malmö**: The national modern art museum's Malmö branch in the old Elverket on Gasverksgatan, with an orange perforated extension. Well known and permanent.
- ★ **Form/Design Center** (m): Design and architecture centre in Hedmanska gården by Lilla Torg, open since 1974 and visited about 100,000 times a year.
- **Orkanenbiblioteket** (m): Malmö University's main library in the Orkanen building by Universitetsholmen. Students and commuters know it, and it is already in the game as Orkanen.
- ★ **Malmö Konsthall**: Klas Anshelm's 1975 hall on S:t Johannesgatan, about 200,000 visitors a year, one of the best-known art venues in the city. OSM tags it amenity=arts_centre, so it was filed under scen. It is already in the game.
- **Malmö Konstmuseum**: The city art museum (wikidata Q2791306, 14 sitelinks). The OSM building is the yellow pavilion in Kungsparken, the former casino, where the museum already runs exhibitions and where the city plans its new art museum (architecture competition 2026–27). ## Kyrkor och gudshus (12)

- ★ **Sankt Petri kyrka**: Malmö's oldest building, 14th-century brick Gothic cathedral-sized church by Stortorget; the city's defining church.
- ★ **Sankt Johannes kyrka**: Jugend church from 1907 with tall copper spires at Sankt Johannesplan by Triangeln; a common directions anchor.
- ★ **Sankt Pauli kyrka**: Large neo-Gothic church in Rörsjöstaden by Kungsgatan, a well-known landmark between Davidshall and Värnhem.
- ★ **Malmö synagoga**: Malmö's 1903 synagogue on Föreningsgatan, historically important and widely known.
- ★ **Malmö moské**: Malmö moské (Islamic Center) at Jägersro/Västra Skrävlinge, Sweden's first purpose-built mosque (1984); same as 'Malmö moské' in landmarks.json.
- ★ **Limhamns kyrka**: Limhamn's parish church with a prominent tower, the landmark of Limhamn.
- **Umm Al-Mu'minin Khadijah-moskén** (m): Umm Al-Mu'minin Khadijah mosque (OSM name: Det Skandinaviska Wakf i Sverige) by Östra kyrkogården, Sweden's largest mosque (2017) and a visible building.
- **Mahmoodmoskén** (m): Mahmoodmoskén in eastern Malmö (2016), Ahmadiyya's largest Swedish mosque with a 24 m minaret, much covered in media.
- **Sankt Andreas kyrka** (m): Sankt Andreas kyrka in Fridhem, 1959 church with a tall free-standing tower, a parish church of S:t Petri; well-known in western Malmö.
- **Hyllie kyrka** (m): Hyllie kyrka, parish church of Hyllie; listed by Visit Malmö and Swedish Wikivoyage.
- **Sankt Matteus kyrka** (m): Sankt Matteus kyrka by Södervärn/Möllevången, a parish church of S:t Johannes with relatively high page views.
- **Kirsebergs kyrka** (m): Kirsebergs kyrka on the Kirseberg ridge, the church of Kirsebergsstaden and visible over north-east Malmö.

> Caroli kyrka (way/34732336, in game) is in byggnad.json, not here; it was deconsecrated in 2010 and sold, so it fits better as a building than a place of worship. Keep it there. The Khadijah mosque's OSM name is the operator ('Det Skandinaviska Wakf i Sverige'); use 'Umm Al-Mu'minin Khadijah-moskén' (or 'Khadijamoskén') as the display name. The long list covers every Svenska kyrkan church in Malmö pastorat except Sankt Nicolai kapell and Syster Astrids kyrka (a room in Husie församlingshem, skipped). Village churches outside Malmö municipality (Vellinge, Svedala, Staffanstorp, Burlöv) were dropped; Burlövs gamla kyrka kept in everything as a medieval church right at the city edge. Owner may prefer to drop it too. Core slots 9-12 (Sankt Andreas, Hyllie, Sankt Matteus, Kirseberg) are judgement calls; Västra Skrävlinge kyrka (by Inre Ringvägen) and Sankta Maria kyrka are the next candidates. Helgeandskyrkan is now Malmö International Church.

## Byggnader och platser (16)

- ★ **Turning Torso**: Malmö's skyline icon, visible from almost everywhere in the city.
- ★ **Malmöhus slott**: The 1500s castle and museum complex everyone knows; anchors Slottsparken.
- ★ **Malmö centralstation**: Malmö C, the main transport hub and the most common meeting point in the city.
- ★ **Triangeln**: Citytunnel station that names the Triangeln area and mall; used constantly for directions.
- ★ **Hyllie**: Citytunnel station at the heart of Hyllie, next to Emporia and Malmö Arena.
- ★ **Malmö universitet** (m): The city university by Centralen; residents know the campus on Universitetsholmen.
- ★ **Malmö Live**: Concert hall, congress centre and hotel tower by Centralen, a skyline landmark since 2015.
- **Rådhuset**: The 1540s town hall on Stortorget, the most recognisable historic facade in central Malmö.
- **Slottsmöllan**: The windmill in Slottsparken, one of the city's most photographed buildings and a common meeting point.
- **Hyllie vattentorn** (m): The mushroom-shaped water tower at Hyllie, visible from the ring road and the bridge approach.
- **Slagthuset** (m): The old slaughterhouse behind Centralen, long known as a nightclub, theatre and event venue.
- **Hotel Kramer** (m): The grand hotel on Stortorget, a classic Malmö reference point.
- **Point Hyllie** (m): Point Hyllie / The Point, at 110 m the second-tallest building in Malmö, visible across the south of the city.
- **Södervärnstornet**: The 1916 water tower on Möllevången, 54 m, visible across Södra Innerstaden and widely known by its rude nickname; Wikidata item not in the long list.
- **Skånes universitetssjukhus (SUS Malmö)**: The city hospital, still called 'MAS' by everyone; a constant direction reference by Södervärn.
- **Kronprinsen**: The 75 m blue 1964 tower block with a mall at its foot; a well known landmark ('vid Kronprinsen'). Already in the handel list as a mall.

## Konst och minnesmärken (9)

- **Karl X Gustav (Stortorget)**: Equestrian statue in the middle of Stortorget since 1896; the city's best-known monument and most-read art article on sv Wikipedia.
- **Optimistorkestern**: Lundell's bronze marching band on Södergatan (not Stortorget or Lilla torg), a standard meeting point on the main pedestrian street.
- **Arbetets ära (Möllevångstorget)**: Axel Ebbe's group of naked workers lifting a granite block, the landmark of Möllevångstorget.
- **Fontänerna på Gustav Adolfs torg (Fem fontäner och ett klot)** (m): Sivert Lindblom's five bronze fountains and granite sphere on Gustav Adolfs torg, burning fire in winter; the square's focal point.
- **Torgbrunn (fontänen på Stortorget)** (m): Stig Blomberg's 1964 bronze fountain on Stortorget, the second fixed point on the main square after the statue.
- **Människan och Pegasus** (m): Carl Milles' winged horse on its tall column at Linnéplatsen by Slottsparken/Stadsbiblioteket, visible from far off and a classic Malmö image.
- **Rubato (Malmö Live)** (m): Eva Hild's six-metre white aluminium loop in front of Malmö Live, the most conspicuous recent work in the city centre.
- **Way to Go (skorna på Davidshallsbron)** (m): Bronze shoes of Malmö performers on a granite red carpet across Davidshallsbron, widely known as 'skorna på Davidshallsbron'.
- **Ekotemplet (Pildammsparken)** (m): The round columned temple from the 1914 Baltic Exhibition in Pildammsparken, a well-known meeting and photo spot.

> The evidence supports a short core (9), not 25: only Karl X Gustav, Optimistorkestern and Arbetets ära have real public signal (Wikipedia views 1-2k); every other work in the list has zero views and almost no web presence. The other six are the fixed points of the main squares and the big centre works people actually point at. Borderline, left in everything: Elma Danielsson agiterar (Rörsjöparken 2023, Malmö's first statue of a named woman, well publicised), Trumslagaren (Lundell's elephant drummer in Folkets park, the 'fifth member' of Optimistorkestern), Frans Suell, Points of View (Tony Cragg at Triangeln), Flyktingmonumentet (Limhamn), Drottningtorgets fontän, Snubbelstenarna. ## Idrott (8)

- **Jägersro**: Sweden's only combined trot and gallop track, a large landmark that names a whole district and a common reference point in eastern Malmö.
- ★ **Eleda Stadion**: Malmö FF's home arena (formerly Swedbank Stadion), the city's football stadium and the most-viewed article in the category.
- ★ **Malmö Arena**: The big indoor arena at Hyllie (Redhawks, Eurovision 2013/2024), next to Hyllie station and a standard reference point there.
- ★ **Baltiska Hallen**: The 1964 sports and event hall on Stadionområdet with the Idrottsmuseet, long the home of handball in Malmö.
- **Malmö Isstadion**: The 1968 ice stadium on Stadionområdet, Redhawks' old home and Eurovision 1992 venue, still in use (IK Pantern).
- ★ **Malmö Idrottsplats**: 'Gamla idrottsplatsen' from 1896 by Pildammsparken/Fersens väg, a central, historic venue everyone passes.
- **Stapelbäddsparken** (m): The concrete skatepark and STPLN culture house in Västra hamnen, a well-known feature of the waterfront since 2005/06.
- ★ **Kockum Fritid**: The big sports and swimming centre in Kockums old industrial building in Västra hamnen; already in the game but missing from the long list (OSM tags it building=sports_centre with no leisure tag).

> Malmö stadion (relation/53037, in landmarks.json) has been demolished: demolition began Nov 2025 and malmo.se says it is finished, with a new 8,000-seat athletics/football stadium being built on the site, due around 2028/29 (https://malmo.se/stadion). Eleda Stadion is the same place as Wikivoyage's 'Swedbank Stadion'. Jägersro is in the game as 'Jägersro travbana' but the long list does not flag it inGame. No 'Malmö Golfhall' exists in OSM; Malmö Indoor Golf in Hyllie is probably it. Malmö Burlöv GK sits on the Malmö/Burlöv border and Malmö City GK Kvarnbybanan is in Malmö (Kvarnby). The other golf clubs (Vallgården, Vellinge, Rönnebäck, Sofiedal) are outside Malmö and dropped. Heleneholms IP and Rosengård norra IP are real but neighbourhood-level, so they are in everything. Bryggeriet and Hyllie Sportcenter are borderline core.

## Handel och marknad (13)

- ★ **Emporia**: Malmö's largest shopping centre at Hyllie, one of the biggest in Scandinavia, with a striking facade by Wingårdh.
- **Triangeln**: The largest shopping centre in central Malmö, built over Triangeln station; 'vid Triangeln' is everyday Malmö orientation.
- ★ **Mobilia**: Large shopping centre with over a hundred shops at Dalaplan/Per Albins väg, in the old Malmö Yllefabrik buildings.
